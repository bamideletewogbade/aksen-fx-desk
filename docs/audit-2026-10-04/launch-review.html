Production readiness

The interface is substantially ahead of the underlying controls. Core trust boundaries and payment confirmation are not implemented safely.

Recommended initial business: Aksen sells workflow software to existing licensed operators. Customers retain their banking, liquidity, compliance and settlement responsibilities. Aksen should not silently become the exchange, custodian, liquidity syndicate manager and software vendor in the same MVP. If the intention is to operate the exchange itself, the licensing, partner, capital, safeguarding and complaint-handling work becomes a much larger separate programme.

The useful differentiation is a reliable corridor-specific operating record: what was quoted, what arrived, who approved payment, what was paid and what remains unreconciled. A chatbot, attractive dashboard or generic AI fraud claim is easy to copy. Trusted integrations, exception handling and repeatable onboarding are harder and more valuable.

  • Interview approximately ten operators; recruit three design partners with a real recurring workflow and an accountable owner.
  • Observe current quoting, incoming-credit checks, payout approval and day-end reconciliation. Measure minutes per trade, rework, unresolved items and errors before assigning ROI.
  • Test a paid setup plus monthly desk subscription. Price provider usage separately or include a clearly bounded allowance. Do not invent a price from unverified transaction volumes.
  • Track gross margin after WhatsApp/provider charges, model usage, payment-data costs and hands-on support. A cheap subscription with hours of onboarding per bureau may be commercially weak.
  • Defer liquidity syndicates and yield marketing. They introduce different buyers, risk and regulatory questions before the core desk is dependable.

5. Architecture: retain the stack, rebuild the trust boundaries

Recommended: a modular Next.js application with Postgres, a durable background worker/queue, private receipt storage and provider adapters. Microservices would add coordination overhead before the domain model is stable. Separate business logic from route handlers and UI state.

Web / approved messaging channel → authenticated API or verified webhook → durable event inbox → domain services and state machine → Postgres transaction + audit event + outbox → worker → bank / messaging adapters → verified callbacks → reconciliation
  • Identity: organisations, users, memberships and roles. Resolve tenant from session membership or verified channel asset, never client-supplied org ID alone. Enforce scope in services and database policies. PostgreSQL owners/bypass roles can bypass RLS, so test with the actual runtime role.
  • Core data: customers, counterparties, quotes, trades, payment observations, evidence objects, approvals, payouts, ledger accounts/entries, audit events, channel installations, webhook inbox and delivery outbox. Keep leads separate from trades; a bureau enquiry must not create a fictitious 1.5m transaction.
  • Money: integer minor units or exact decimal storage; explicit ISO currency and decimal rate precision; documented rounding. Store offered input/output/fees and expiry immutably. Do not use binary floating arithmetic for final ledger amounts.
  • Concurrency: tenant-scoped unique external references, idempotency keys and guarded version transitions. Persist transition plus event/outbox in a short database transaction. Call providers outside locks. Retry safely after timeouts and distinguish unknown outcome from failure.
  • Accounting: append-only balanced entries per currency with explicit FX clearing accounts; separate actual, reserved and available balances. Correct through reversals, not overwritten balances. Reconcile provider/bank references and statements daily. A click cannot prove reconciliation.
  • Evidence: private uploads with type/size checks, scanning, access controls, signed links, retention and access logs. Bank-credit records and payout confirmations are authoritative; screenshots are supporting evidence.
  • AI: propose extracted fields or explain records; preserve provenance and uncertainty; schema-validate outputs and require human handling for ambiguity. Keep the model out of authorization, payment truth and ledger decisions.
  • Operations: structured logs without sensitive payloads, correlation IDs, alerts for stalled trades/webhooks/reconciliation, tenant-aware rate limits, secret encryption/rotation, backups and a tested restore process.

Target process and exceptions

Draft → quoted → accepted / awaiting funds → funds confirmed → review and approval → payout pending → payout confirmed → reconciled

Alongside that happy path, model expired/cancelled quotes, underpayment, overpayment, unmatched credits, insufficient float, rejected reviews, pending/failed/unknown payouts, disputes and refund pending/refunded. Keep payment evidence state separate from trade and payout state. Support needs an accountable owner and an escalation path for every stuck state.

Use maker/checker controls at policy-defined thresholds, with no self-approval where separation is required. Start with operator-controlled verification and payout recording if bank APIs are unavailable, but require reference, evidence and reconciliation. “Manual” is a legitimate scope; pretending it is automated is the defect.

Hosting recommendation

For this existing Next.js project, Vercel plus the existing Neon direction is the lowest-migration starting point, subject to required data region and service terms. Cloudflare also requires a complete server runtime; the HTML-copy script cannot host the application’s APIs. Current Cloudflare guidance points to vinext and documents OpenNext as an alternative, so evaluate that migration deliberately rather than mixing deployment approaches. See Vercel and Cloudflare.

6. WhatsApp: the lowest-friction route is conditional

Resolve the product’s eligibility first. The WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy restricts facilitating currency transactions. The proposed FX quote-to-settlement experience therefore has a direct policy conflict to resolve; a licence or BSP does not itself exempt it. Obtain a written assessment of the exact workflow before promising this distribution channel. Do not use unofficial QR-session automation to avoid review.

If the approved use case permits it, start with BSP-assisted hosted/embedded onboarding and Coexistence for eligible existing WhatsApp Business App numbers. This can let a customer keep their familiar app while adding an API-connected desk. Eligibility, message/history behavior and migration limits vary; do not promise every number can connect.

What the customer should do

  1. Create their Aksen organisation and complete business eligibility checks with an accountable administrator.
  2. Select “Connect WhatsApp”, enter a hosted provider/Meta authorization flow, choose their business assets and authorize the eligible number. The customer owns their number and business assets.
  3. Where supported, follow the provider's Coexistence steps; otherwise make an explicit migration/new-number decision. Do not ask every bureau to copy developer tokens into a form.
  4. Aksen stores encrypted installation credentials, maps phone-number ID to tenant, subscribes webhooks and verifies connectivity.
  5. Configure allowed templates, consent/opt-out handling and a human inbox; complete a controlled inbound/reply/status test before showing “Connected”.
  6. Train the operator, publish service expectations, document disconnection/reconnection and export/migration ownership.

Budget the platform subscription, Meta/provider messaging fees, AI usage, verification services and support. No current prices were independently validated. The policy includes a 24-hour service window and template rules outside it; build these into delivery logic, not operator memory. For sensitive documents and identifiers, use an appropriately secured web flow and assess policy/privacy requirements.

What established products teach us

  • Uber: its official 2022 India launch described a narrow booking flow with pickup/drop-off, upfront fare, ETA and support tied to a real ride backend. Learn the bounded task and operational handoff; this is a historical example, not a claim of current worldwide availability. Uber case
  • JioMart / Haptik: guided commerce connects browsing, cart, address and payment. Learn structured steps backed by actual order state. Vendor-reported success metrics are not evidence for Aksen or FX eligibility. Provider case
  • OpenAI: its official transition notice states ChatGPT on WhatsApp ended after 15 January 2026. The relevant lesson is channel dependence: keep customer identity, records and core workflow portable. OpenAI notice

Build the valuable desk first, with WhatsApp as an adapter if permitted. If the exact transaction flow is ineligible, reassess an independently compliant product/channel scope; moving the same prohibited facilitation behind a link is not an automatic solution.
