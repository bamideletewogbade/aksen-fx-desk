Voice rules

  • No em dashes. No "AI-powered", "leverage", "solution", "streamline".
  • No absolutes: no "never misses", "replaces your staff", "guaranteed growth".
  • No invented customers, logos or numbers. TFS is not public proof.
  • Plain words a shop owner uses. Sound like a sharp local operator, not an agency.

How ideas become products

  1. Spot the process, not the idea. Every build starts from a workflow someone already runs.
  2. Build the working version to win the work. Previews are persuasion artifacts.
  3. Same shape every time: customer surface + back office + agent on the business's messaging channel + human-in-the-loop approvals.
  4. Docs are the source of truth. Implementation statements, never launch claims.
  5. Prices are invented until validated, and marked so.

The scoreboard

Two numbers only: conversations with owners this week and cedis invoiced this month. A sent message with no reply is not a conversation. A week with zero owner conversations is a failed week however much shipped.

The differentiator

"We don't just build websites, apps or dashboards. We build digital versions of your physical processes, digitizing each workflow where we can, with humans kept in the loop for the decisions that matter." The market promises autonomous agents. We sell the boundary: AI prepares, a person decides, enforced by the system.

Rules for every session

  • Read the venture's own brief before proposing anything.
  • Evidence over enthusiasm. "Not found" is a finding.
  • Never send anything to a prospect autonomously. Drafts only, always.
  • Protect the build-to-sell ratio. Code without a conversation is a worse week, not a better one.
