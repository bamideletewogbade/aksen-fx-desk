## 1. The line we sell with

This is the frame for every conversation, written once so it stays consistent:

> **"We don't start from a website. We start from the process your business already runs: the enquiries, the quotes, the orders, the approvals. We build the digital version of that process, and where a decision needs a person, a person makes it. Software carries the work. You keep the judgment."**

Why this frame wins with this list: every one of these businesses already runs a physical process (DM in, price out, deposit paid, frame built, delivered). They don't need to be sold "AI" or "a website". They need their existing process to stop leaking. The human-in-the-loop line is also the honest differentiator: the software cannot set a price, take a payment or promise a date. That restriction is a feature to lead with, not a limitation to hide.

Words that are banned in anything we send: "AI-powered", "leverage", "solution", "streamline", "revolutionize", "never misses", "guaranteed". No em dashes anywhere in copy.

## 6. The email question, answered

**Do not hand over your Gmail, and no, we don't need a cold-email platform.**

1. **Your personal Gmail stays personal.** At 60 prospects over weeks, you send messages yourself, one at a time. That is not a limitation, it is the strategy: replies to a person convert better than replies to a campaign, and the hard rule already says a person decides what goes out. Automated cold-email tools (Instantly, Smartlead) want dedicated domains, warmup cycles and $40–100/month, and none of that is justified under 100 sends.
2. **The channel order for this list is not email anyway.** These businesses run on Instagram DM and WhatsApp. Email is the fourth channel here, behind LinkedIn for the 8 named people and behind a physical visit for Accra.
3. **The platform's own email loop needs Resend, and it's free.** The enquiry outbox is built and switched off waiting for `RESEND_API_KEY` and `RESEND_FROM_EMAIL`. Resend's free tier covers it (about 3,000 emails a month). Two notes: the test address can only mail your own account, so a real from-address needs one verified domain. Right now the public link is a workers.dev subdomain; buying one real domain (something like aksenlabs.com) unlocks both the verified email sender and a credible link for outreach. That is the one small spend worth making.
4. **Free tools exist if you want a separate sending identity later** (Zoho Mail free tier with your own domain, Gmail aliases), but the honest answer at this stage is: one domain, Resend for the site, your own hand for outreach.

## 7. Jev-type gates (calibrated yes/no calls)

Where a yes/no judgment model fits the engagement machinery: is this incoming DM an order enquiry or a casual scroll (routes reply priority)? Does this prospect's message mention a framing job (auto-draft vs ignore)? Where it does not fit: prices and size matches are plain code, and a frame photo needs a description before any judgment. Only worth building after the first Order Desk client exists; not now.

## 8. Weekly rhythm

- **Monday:** pick 10 Tier 1/2 prospects for the week. Send day-0 openers (max 5/day, hand-touched).
- **Daily:** one-message floor from the Business OS applies here too. SELL lane = this list.
- **Friday:** update the pipeline, count conversations with owners. A week with zero owner conversations is a failed week regardless of what shipped.
- Every reply lands in the pipeline with source tag. The Business Check booking is the conversion event; everything before it is postcard, not conversation.
