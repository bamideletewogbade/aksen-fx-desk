## How ideas become products (observed pattern)

1. **Spot the process, not the idea.** Every build starts from a physical workflow someone already runs: framing orders by WhatsApp (TFS), rentals (ProptisGH), CVs and job applications (CV Forge). The question is always "what does this person actually do all day", never "what could we build".
2. **Build the working version before the agreement.** TFS app-mvp exists "to win the work rather than because the work is agreed." Previews are persuasion artifacts. Risk noted in his own docs: build-ahead-of-alignment.
3. **Every product gets the same shape:** customer-facing surface (shop/portal) + back office (orders, quotes, money, production) + AI agent on the messaging channel where the business already lives (WhatsApp) + human-in-the-loop approvals. The agent prepares; the person prices, takes payment, promises dates. Enforced in code, not just policy.
4. **Docs as the source of truth.** Each venture carries a README/operating brief that states honestly what exists vs what is claimed. "Implementation statements, not a production launch claim." Facts change in the doc first, then the code.
5. **Pricing is invented until validated, and he says so.** Every price in the TFS MVP is marked invented. The Nigeria kit config centralises prices in one file with a comment: "a price that lives only on an image a friend has already posted cannot be taken back."

## How he thinks about selling (from the operating brief)

- **Scoreboard is two numbers only:** conversations with owners this week; cedis invoiced this month. Features shipped is deliberately not on it: "all three can be at an all-time high in a company that is dying."
- **Business-OS:** three things a day (SELL first, BUILD, COMPOUND), pre-decided queue, 25-minute items, one-message floor on bad days, append-only daily log. Markdown files and slash commands, not dashboards.
- **Known failure mode (self-diagnosed):** building instead of selling. The 12 Sept review said it plainly; it is the single most important line to hold him to. SELL is never optional.
- **The ladder:** free Assessment (since 28 Sept) → Build from GHS 6,500 → Operate GHS 900–18,000/mo. Nigeria kit prices in naira (Business Check free, Lead Pack ₦29,900, Starter Page ₦99,000, Order Desk ₦149,000, Bookings ₦179,000, custom from ₦450,000).
- **Positioning (his words to keep):** "We don't just build websites, apps or dashboards. We build digital versions of your physical processes, digitizing each workflow where we can, with humans kept in the loop for the decisions that matter." This is the HITL architecture pitch and it is genuinely differentiating: the market is full of people promising autonomous agents; he sells the boundary.

## Voice rules (enforced everywhere)

- No em dashes. No AI-stock phrasing (delve, leverage, in today's fast-paced world, it's not just X it's Y).
- Plain words a shop owner uses. "Digital transformation" is the category, not the opener.
- No absolutes (never misses, replaces staff, guaranteed growth). No invented customers, logos, percentages. TFS is not public proof.
- Copy should sound like a sharp local operator, not an agency. Short sentences. Specific detail beats adjectives.
